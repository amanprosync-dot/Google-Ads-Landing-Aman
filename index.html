About the Model

How Amazon FBA Works

Fulfilment by Amazon (FBA) is a service that allows sellers to store inventory in Amazon's warehouses. Amazon then manages storage, packaging, shipping, and customer service on the seller's behalf.

📦

Inventory Management

Products are stored in Amazon's fulfilment centres. Amazon manages stock and pick-and-pack operations.

🚚

Shipping & Delivery

Amazon handles labelling, dispatch, and last-mile delivery through its own logistics infrastructure.

🧾

Billing & Returns

Amazon manages invoicing, GST-compliant billing, and customer return processing as part of the FBA service.

🌐

Platform Access

FBA sellers gain visibility on Amazon's marketplace and Prime eligibility for their listed products.

📊

Seller Dashboard

Amazon provides a centralised interface to monitor sales, inventory, performance metrics, and payouts.

🔧

Remote Operation

FBA operations can be managed remotely. Sellers are not required to maintain a physical warehouse or in-house logistics team.
