Privacy Policy & Data Protection Notice

Last updated: 14 November 2026

ProSync Infotech Pvt. Ltd. ("ProSync", "we", "us" or "our") is the Data Fiduciary for personal data collected through this webinar page. This notice explains, in line with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 ("DPDP Act") and the rules made under it, what personal data we process, why, and the rights you have.

1. Personal data we process

  • Registration and payment details. When you register, you are taken to our payment partner, Instamojo, where you provide your name, email address, phone number and payment details. We receive your name, email, phone number and the status of your payment. We do not see or store your card, UPI or bank credentials.
  • Messages you send us. If you email or call us, we keep the details you share so we can reply.
  • Technical information. Our hosting provider and the third-party services below may automatically receive your IP address, browser type, device and pages visited.
  • Advertising and measurement data. We use Google Ads conversion tracking and, where enabled, Google Analytics and similar advertising tags. These tools use cookies and similar identifiers to record that a visit came from an advertisement, which pages and buttons were used, and whether a registration or payment was completed. We use this to measure and improve our advertising and website. We do not send your name, email or phone number to these tools through this page. See section 4 for how to control them.

2. Purposes and legal basis

We process your personal data only for the purposes below, on the basis of your consent or for the limited "legitimate uses" the DPDP Act allows (for example, performing the service you asked for, or complying with law).

  • To register you for the webinar and send joining details and reminders (consent, given when you register).
  • To process payments, issue receipts and prevent fraud (consent and legal obligation).
  • To respond to your questions and provide support.
  • To tell you about future webinars, programs and offers (only if you separately agree; this is optional and you may say no without losing access to the webinar).
  • To maintain, secure and improve this website.

3. Consent and how to withdraw it

Before you are taken to the registration form, we ask you to confirm that you have read this notice. Your consent is free, specific and informed, and you may withdraw it at any time, as easily as you gave it, by emailing us at the address in section 11. Withdrawal does not affect processing done before it, and we may need to keep certain records (such as payment records) where the law requires. Withdrawing consent for a purpose may mean we can no longer provide the related service.

4. Third-party services and data processors

  • Instamojo for payments and the registration form. Instamojo processes your data on our behalf and under its own privacy policy.
  • Email, messaging and meeting-platform providers that help us send joining details and run the session.
  • Instagram and LinkedIn links. These platforms may set cookies or collect data if you visit them.
  • Google Fonts, which loads fonts from Google's servers and therefore receives your IP address.
  • Google Ads and Google Analytics (Google LLC) for advertising measurement and website analytics, using cookies and similar identifiers. You can manage ad personalisation at adssettings.google.com, opt out of Analytics with the Google Analytics opt-out add-on, or block or delete cookies in your browser settings. Blocking them does not stop you from registering for the webinar.

We do not sell your personal data. We share it only with processors bound to protect it, when required by law or a government authority, or to protect our rights, users or the public. Some of these providers may store data outside India; we transfer data only where the DPDP Act permits.

5. Data retention

We keep your personal data only as long as needed for the purposes above and to meet legal, accounting and tax obligations. Payment and invoice records are kept for the period required by Indian tax law. Other data, including marketing contact details, is deleted or anonymised once the purpose is served or you withdraw consent.

6. Security and data breaches

We take reasonable security safeguards to protect your data. If a personal data breach affects you, we will inform you and the Data Protection Board of India as the law requires.

7. Your rights

Under the DPDP Act you have the right to:

  • get a summary of the personal data we process and who we share it with;
  • correct inaccurate or incomplete data, and update or erase data that is no longer needed;
  • withdraw your consent at any time;
  • have your grievance redressed (section 9); and
  • nominate another person to exercise these rights on your behalf if you die or become incapacitated.

To exercise any right, email us at the address in section 11. We will respond within 30 days.

8. Children

This webinar is for adults. We do not knowingly process the personal data of anyone under 18 and do not track or target children. If we learn that a child's data has been provided without verifiable consent of a parent or guardian, we will delete it.
